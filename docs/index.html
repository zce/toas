The short version

One gesture.
Three quiet steps.

toas stays out of the way until you need it. Hold the shortcut, talk, release. Cloud processing handles the heavy work; the local layer simply gets out of your way.

01

Hold to talk

Keep your hands on the keyboard with the default Ctrl + Shift + Space shortcut. A live waveform confirms that toas is listening.

02

Transcribe, then refine

Audio becomes text through your configured service. Optional Refine removes filler and fixes punctuation without changing what you meant.

03

Land where you are

Single-line fields get direct text input. Multiline text uses a safe clipboard path, with terminal-aware paste and a target-window safeguard.

Privacy, clearly bounded

We keep the local layer
small and visible.

Privacy has two sides. toas is explicit about what happens on your machine and what happens after an API call.

01 / ON YOUR DESKTOP

Nothing listens in the background.

No telemetry, context capture, local model runtime, or always-on inference service. Audio is captured only while you record and sent only after you stop.

toas controlscapture / storage / output
02 / AFTER UPLOAD

Your provider sets the policy.

toas sends completed audio to the transcription endpoint you configure. Optional Refine sends transcript text to its own endpoint. Retention, training, logging, and residency depend on that provider.

you chooseendpoint / model / policy
03 / YOUR CONTROL

Keep, limit, or clear.

Your words and retained recordings stay on your machine for history and retry. Set retention in Preferences, use environment variables for keys, and clear history when you want.
