The whole idea

Speak where you type.

One shortcut, one small overlay, and then you are back where you started.

How it works

Hold. Speak. Release.

toas stays quiet until you ask for it. No extra window, no context switching, no local model runtime to keep alive.

01

Record

Hold Ctrl + Shift + Space and speak. Release the modifiers when you are done.

02

Process

Your completed recording goes to the transcription provider you chose. Refine can optionally clean up the result.

03

Insert

The result returns to the focused app. If the target changes while processing, the text stays safely on your clipboard.

record→transcribe→optional refine→insert

Your setup

Choose the stack that fits you.

Everything important lives in GNOME Preferences. Pick a transcription provider, add its API key, optionally enable Refine, and add useful Context when the provider supports it.

Privacy

Small local layer.
Clear cloud boundary.

toas does not hide what leaves your machine. The local behavior is controlled by the extension; provider-side handling follows the service you choose.

01

No background listening

Audio is captured only while you explicitly record and uploaded only after you stop.

02

Your provider, its policy

Completed audio goes to your transcription provider. Optional Refine sends transcript text to its configured provider.

03

Local history, your control

History and retained recordings stay local. Private mode disables retention for new voice inputs during the session.
