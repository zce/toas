How it works

Hold. Speak. Release.

Toas stays out of the way until you use your shortcut. No separate app or local AI model to run.

01

Record

Hold Ctrl + Shift + Space and speak. Release the modifiers when you are done.

02

Process

Your completed recording goes to the transcription provider you chose. Refine can optionally clean up the result.

03

Insert

The result returns to the focused app. If the target changes while processing, the text stays safely on your clipboard.

record→transcribe→optional refine→insert

Privacy

Small local layer.
Clear cloud boundary.

Audio is recorded locally until you stop. Once uploaded, your chosen providers' privacy policies apply.

01

No background listening

Audio is captured only while you explicitly record and uploaded only after you stop.

02

Your provider, its policy

Completed audio goes to your transcription provider. Optional Refine sends transcript text to its configured provider.

03

Local history, your control

History and retained recordings stay local. Private mode disables retention for new voice inputs until you turn it off.
